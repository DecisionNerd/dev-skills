## What it is

Recover vision and situation; pick one achievable goal before tools. A strategy is a claim about what kind of progress this moment owes the project — then a recognisable set of [practices](../practices/index.md) that tend to earn it.

## Why it works

Without a named strategy, teams mix shipping, rewriting, and tidying in one thrashy session. Naming the strategy constrains tool choice and makes stop conditions obvious.

## When to use it

When lost, idk, or starting a session cold. Not for Sev-1 prod (contain first).
