## What it is

Keep agent systems sharp: design and optimize when calm; slap and drain when on fire. A strategy is a claim about what kind of progress this moment owes the project — then a recognisable set of [practices](../practices/index.md) that tend to earn it.

## Why it works

Without a named strategy, teams mix shipping, rewriting, and tidying in one thrashy session. Naming the strategy constrains tool choice and makes stop conditions obvious.

## When to use it

Thrash, loops, dumb workflows — or greenfield agent design. Never optimize a runaway; slap first.
