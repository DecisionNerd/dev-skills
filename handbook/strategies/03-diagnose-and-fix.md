# Diagnose and Fix

Find the real failure, plan the repair, implement the smallest fix that restores the promise.

**Prerequisite:** [Orientation](../orientation/index.md). Related: [This is Fine](../concepts/01-this-is-fine-stance.md), [Smallest Next Step](../concepts/03-smallest-next-step.md), [Vision-Tied Goals](../concepts/08-vision-tied-goals.md).

## What it is

Find the real failure, plan the repair, implement the smallest fix that restores the promise. A strategy is a claim about what kind of progress this moment owes the project — then a recognisable set of [practices](../practices/index.md) that tend to earn it.

## Why it works

Without a named strategy, teams mix shipping, rewriting, and tidying in one thrashy session. Naming the strategy constrains tool choice and makes stop conditions obvious.

## When to use it

User-visible or data-plane breakage; backend/algo bugs. Don’t “fix” by spawning swarms.
