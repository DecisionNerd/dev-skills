## What it is

Improve structure, proof, visibility, and docs so the next session is calmer. A strategy is a claim about what kind of progress this moment owes the project — then a recognisable set of [practices](../practices/index.md) that tend to earn it.

## Why it works

Without a named strategy, teams mix shipping, rewriting, and tidying in one thrashy session. Naming the strategy constrains tool choice and makes stop conditions obvious.

## When to use it

After the fire is contained, or when vision demands quality. Not endless polish as avoidance.
