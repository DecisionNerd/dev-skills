# Agent Agency

Who decides, who writes, who must ask — for humans and subagents.

## Definition

Agency is control with responsibility. Parent agents orchestrate; children get bounded briefs; humans approve mutating prod, merges, and destructive tidy-up.

## Why it matters

Overlapping writers and silent prod mutations are how rooms ignite. Clear agency is Fail Safe for teams-with-agents.

## For engineers and agents

- One write owner per path set
- `agents sub` for parallel *explore*; sequential mutate
- `agents slap` when agency collapses into thrash
