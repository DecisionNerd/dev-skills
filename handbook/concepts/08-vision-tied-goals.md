# Vision-Tied Goals

Session or weekly goals that visibly advance why the project exists — not mere motion.

## Definition

A vision-tied goal connects an achievable outcome to the user/maintainer promise. “Refactor utils” is rarely vision-tied unless it unblocks a promised capability or a burning reliability fire.

## Why it matters

It’s how `idk-now` refuses busywork and how strategies stay honest.

## For engineers and agents

- State vision (even provisional) before the goal
- Reject goals that only rearrange smoke
- Prefer shipping a thin slice of the promise over perfect internals
