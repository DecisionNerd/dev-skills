# Smallest Next Step

The least work that creates real progress toward a vision-tied goal — finishable in one focused session (or clearly “part 1”).

## Definition

Smallest next step is anti-boil-the-ocean. It is not “the easiest busywork.” Cleaning a worktree that blocks shipping can be the smallest *real* step; rewriting the architecture usually isn’t.

## Why it matters

Large vague goals guarantee thrash. Small real steps compound and leave designed endings.

## For engineers and agents

- State the step in one sentence before tools
- If the step needs more than one overlapping writer, split ownership
- Prefer `recon issue` plans with sequenced steps over a mega-PR
