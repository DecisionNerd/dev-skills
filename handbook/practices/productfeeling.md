# ProductFeeling

Emotion-aware product design companion — feeling north star and audits.

## What it is

Emotion-aware product design companion — feeling north star and audits. This practice is a skill-mapped TTP: *when*, *why*, and *which command*—not a full copy of the skill. Open the skill to execute.

## Why it works

Emotion and usability are coupled (Norman); peak–end memory shapes what users retain (Kahneman/NN/g). Feeling north stars make craft and metrics accountable to experience, not only funnels.

## When to use it

When the situation matches the one-liner above and [Orientation](../orientation/index.md) (or your [project path](../paths/index.md)) says this is the fire to touch now.

## Do

- Invoke the skill; follow its safety rules
- Keep one write owner; collect evidence before claiming done
- Hand off to the next practice instead of boiling the ocean

## Don't

- Skip orientation when you’re lost
- Spawn overlapping agents to “go faster”
- Treat the practice as done without evidence
