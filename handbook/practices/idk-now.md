# IDK Now

When you don’t know what to do next: survey, ask, suggest a vision-tied next skill.

## What it is

When you don’t know what to do next: survey, ask, suggest a vision-tied next skill. This practice is a skill-mapped TTP: *when*, *why*, and *which command*—not a full copy of the skill. Open the skill to execute.

## Why it works

Under uncertainty, generating options without a goal increases thrash. Product and engineering research both start from problem framing and user/outcome clarity before solutions. A short survey + constrained next step mirrors small-batch delivery and Beck’s “pass the tests / reveal intention” bias toward clarity.

## When to use it

When the situation matches the one-liner above and [Orientation](../orientation/index.md) (or your [lifecycle](../flow/index.md) / [Architecture](../architecture/index.md) bet) says this is the fire to touch now.

## Do

- Invoke the skill; follow its safety rules
- Keep one write owner; collect evidence before claiming done
- Hand off to the next practice instead of boiling the ocean

## Don't

- Default to DocSlime init when a surgical `document-it` or in-chat vision statement unblocks
- Skip orientation when you’re lost
- Spawn overlapping agents to “go faster”
- Treat the practice as done without evidence
